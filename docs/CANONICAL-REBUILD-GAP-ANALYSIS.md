## Proposed information architecture

1. **Entry** — one brief stair-mark climb; skip at any time; reduced-motion users arrive directly.
2. **Read** — curated library and prepared arrivals; each card has the author's clickable portrait/username and provenance where relevant.
3. **Write** — a quiet, local-first pad with only title, body and optional category/preview/credits; primary actions are Share, Rehearse and Save.
4. **Rehearse** — full-screen stage, paced controls and a calm exit summary/take/private note.
5. **Author's Corner** — `/:username` for people and `/official/:slug` for vetted source/system accounts; each corner lists its published work and the same badge identity.
6. **Admin Suite** — existing `/admin`, organized into overview/health, people & profiles, badges/audit, official accounts, presentations/moderation, arrivals/source queue, metrics, and retained site/setup desks.

The public navigation stays small; administration and profile settings remain contextual, not additional primary destinations.

## Proposed ownership and data model

| System | Authoritative records | Deliberately not authoritative for |
|---|---|---|
| Clerk | Sign-in identity and session subject (`uid`) | Profile, username directory, badges, presentation bodies |
| Supabase | `profiles`, `handles`, social relationships/activity evidence, `admins`, official-account directory, badge assignments and immutable badge-audit events | Full presentation text, drafts, presentation versions |
| Turso | `library` (canonical published presentation), `presentation_versions`, deduplicated public read counts, curated arrival/provenance records | User identity and badge ownership |
| Convex | Bounded safety snapshots/repair markers only; enforce `floor(published / 100)` selected personal presentations and at most 1% of each selected body's UTF-8 bytes | Live presence/activity, canonical reads/writes, full-library replication |
| Browser storage | Private drafts, rehearsal takes/notes and preference state until the user chooses to share | Public/shared content or another user's private writing |

### Important decisions

- **Separate person and official namespaces.** A source account is not a fake Clerk user and cannot collide with a claimed username; `/official/:slug` makes its status and canonical source explicit.
- **One content writer.** A share/edit/version transaction goes to Turso. Supabase may hold the small identity/social references needed to govern it, but not another editable copy of the body. Convex is an opt-in, capped safety copy, never the read fallback.
- **Badge ownership is distinct from presentation display cache.** There are exactly three public badges. `Quintal` is permanent after 100 presentations written and 100 presentations read/rehearsed; `Sole` is permanent for every person who ever held the top read/view count, with no first award before someone reaches 10,000; `Grey` is exclusive to vetted official/source accounts (system-source accounts receive it by nature). Supabase owns active assignments and the audit trail, but eligibility automation and public resolution are not yet complete.
- **Arrivals are prepared, not replenished by the reader.** A scheduled/admin refresh creates candidates with source URL, licence, fetched time and cleaned text; a curator can approve/override before publication. A missed schedule should show the last good batch and a clear health state, not a broken five-item promise.
- **Metadata restraint is intentional.** Category, preview and credits remain optional; author is derived from the signed-in identity only on Share. Occasion, difficulty, audience and forced tags are excluded from the writing interaction.

This is a status snapshot, not a completion claim. Preserve the existing SEO/security/PWA behavior and frozen Vercel environment contract while closing the listed product and verification gaps.
